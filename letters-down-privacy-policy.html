Privacy Policy for Letters Down

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

Many Blessings Apps provides Letters Down, a word puzzle game available on Android and in web browsers. This policy explains what information is handled when you play. The browser version does not display ads or offer in-game purchases. The Android app may display ads and offers an optional one-time purchase to remove them.

Playing and Device Storage

You can play solo without creating an account. The game stores puzzle progress, preferences, and, when you sign in, your session on your device using the app or browser's local storage. Clearing the app's or browser's stored data removes information kept only on that device. If you use an account, game progress and friend matches can sync between Android and web browsers.

Accounts and Usernames

An account is optional and is used for username-based friend challenges. To create one, you provide a username and password; the game does not ask for an email address or phone number. We create a random account identifier and store the username, a salted password hash, and account creation and sign-in session information. We do not store your password in readable form. Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days.

Your username can be found by someone who knows and searches for it. It is shown to people you challenge and to accepted friends. The game does not have public profile pages or chat.

Friend Challenges and Game Information

To operate accounts and matches, our game service stores usernames and account identifiers, friend and block relationships, challenge and rematch codes and statuses, match participants, guesses, clue use, attempt counts, results, and relevant timestamps. If you choose to share a one-time match invitation, anyone with that link may be able to open the invitation.

We use this information to provide sign-in, friend challenges, cross-device matches, account security, and customer support. We do not sell this information.

Advertising and Ad-Free Purchase on Android

Advertising is used only in the Android app. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share the device's IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers. An IP address may be used to estimate general location. Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance. The app uses Google's consent tools where required and provides ad privacy options when they are available.

The Android app offers an optional one-time purchase to remove ads. Google Play processes the payment and provides the app with purchase status so it can enable or restore the ad-free feature. We do not receive your payment card details through the app. Google Play handles payment details and transaction records under its own privacy practices.

Service Providers

We use hosting and database services to operate the game and store account and match information. The Android app also uses Google Mobile Ads, Google's consent tools, and Google Play Billing. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and under their own privacy terms. The web browser version does not use the Google advertising or billing services.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure for more about Google's practices.

Security

Information sent between the game and its service is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are processed using a salted, one-way hash before storage. We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information handled by the game.

Data Retention and Deletion

We keep account information and associated game records while needed to provide the account and match features. You can delete your username account from the Friend Board in the game or through the account deletion page. Deleting an account removes that account and its associated server-stored friends, challenges, matches, and related records. Information already copied or displayed on another player's device may remain there.

If you played a match without an account and want associated server-stored match information removed, email us with enough detail to identify the match. You may also clear local game data from your browser or device. Google manages information collected by its advertising and billing services under its own retention and deletion practices.

Children

Letters Down is intended for people age 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. The game does not ask for a player's date of birth. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will take steps to delete the account and its associated information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the game or its data practices change. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
