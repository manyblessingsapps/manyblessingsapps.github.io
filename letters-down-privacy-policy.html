Privacy Policy for Letters Down

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

Many Blessings Apps provides Letters Down, a word puzzle game with advertising, optional username accounts for friend challenges, and a one-time purchase to remove ads. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use Letters Down on Android or the web.

Accounts and Usernames

Solo play does not require an account. To find and challenge other players by username, you may create an account using a username and password. No phone number or email address is required. We store your username, an account identifier, and a salted password hash so you can sign in on Android and the web. The password itself is not stored in readable form.

Friend Challenges and Game Data

When you use username challenges, Letters Down stores your saved friends, challenge codes, the players in each match, your guesses, guess counts, clue use, and match results on our game service so the match can continue across Android and web devices. Your username is shown to the person you challenge and to accepted friends. The game does not include chat or public profiles. You can also choose to share a one-time invite link outside the app.

Daily and practice game progress is stored locally on your device. Clearing the app or browser storage removes that local data.

Advertising

Letters Down uses Google Mobile Ads to show advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information such as your device’s IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers. An IP address may be used to estimate a device’s general location. Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance.

Google’s advertising and consent choices may vary based on your location, device settings, and consent choices.

One-Time Ad-Free Purchase

Letters Down offers an optional one-time purchase to remove ads. Google Play processes the payment. The app receives purchase status from Google Play so it can provide or restore the ad-free feature. Many Blessings Apps does not receive your payment card details through the app. Google Play handles payment information and transaction records under its own terms and privacy practices.

Third-Party Services

Letters Down uses Google Mobile Ads and Google Play Billing, services provided by Google. Google may collect and process information according to its own privacy policy and terms. Our hosting and database providers process game account and match information to operate the service. For more information about Google’s practices, see Google’s Privacy Policy.

Data Security

Account and game information is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are transformed into salted hashes before they are stored. Information transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is also encrypted in transit.

Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your username account and its server-stored friends, challenges, and match data from the Friend Board in the game. You can also start account deletion at the Letters Down account deletion page. Account deletion removes the account and associated server records. Copies of information already saved on another player’s device may remain there. Google manages information collected through its advertising and billing services under Google’s own retention and deletion practices.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
